There’s an unspoken question most of us carry into work every day: Does my work here actually matter? It’s a deeply human need to feel significant to those around us—to know we’re valued and that we add value. When that feeling is missing, the effects ripple outward: disengagement, disconnection, and a diminishing sense of self-worth.
